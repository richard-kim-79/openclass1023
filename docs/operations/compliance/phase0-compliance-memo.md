# Phase 0 Compliance Memo

**Prepared by:** Compliance Program Manager  
**Date:** 2024-04-10  
**Distribution:** Legal, Operations, Product Leadership

## Purpose
Document the governance approach for Phase 0 pilots, ensuring privacy, copyright, and accessibility obligations are met before broader rollout.

## Scope
- Pilot deployments with up to five partner schools.
- Learner dashboard, instructor analytics, and content authoring surfaces touched during pilot.

## Approval Workflow
1. **Drafting (Owner: Compliance Program Manager):** Consolidate regulatory obligations, confirm mitigation owners, and assemble evidence packets.
2. **Legal Review (Owner: Privacy Counsel):** Validate consent artifacts, cross-border transfer rationale, and copyright inventories. Target turnaround: 5 business days.
3. **Operations Review (Owner: Regional Compliance Lead):** Confirm execution readiness for manual SOPs (consent logging, moderation queue, accessibility audits).
4. **Executive Sign-off (Owner: COO):** Approve pilot launch upon closure of all identified gaps and documentation of residual risks.

### Escalation Path
- Urgent blockers escalate to the COO and General Counsel within 24 hours.
- Non-urgent gaps logged in the risk register and reviewed in weekly compliance stand-up.

## Documentation & Evidence Storage Policies
- Store signed consent forms and moderation decisions in the secure compliance SharePoint library (`/Compliance/Pilot-Phase0/`).
- Restrict access to Compliance, Legal, and Operations leadership groups; enforce MFA and quarterly access reviews.
- Archive accessibility audit findings and remediation tickets in Jira project `ACC-OPS`, linking to the SharePoint evidence folder.
- Retain all Phase 0 records for 3 years or until superseded by Phase 1 controls, whichever is longer.

## Risk Tracking & Gap Management
- Map every obligation in the Phase 0 roadmap to the [risk register extract](../risk-register.md) to monitor mitigation health.
- Confirm gap owners update mitigation status during the weekly compliance stand-up and log changes in the risk register.
- Escalate unresolved gaps that threaten launch readiness to the COO and General Counsel for decision within 24 hours.

## Monitoring & Reporting
- Weekly compliance status updates during the pilot window, highlighting gap remediation progress and risk register changes.
- Post-pilot retrospective to confirm closure of temporary manual processes and hand-off to scaled solutions in Phase 1.
