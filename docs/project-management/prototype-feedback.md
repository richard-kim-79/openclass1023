# Prototype Feedback Plan

## Objective
Select the immediate follow-up work after delivering the Phase 0 clickable prototype and establish a feedback loop that informs backlog prioritization before engineering begins.

## Next Task Selection Workshop
- **Facilitator:** Product Design Lead
- **Participants:** Compliance Program Manager, Engineering Manager, Legal Privacy Counsel, UX Accessibility Specialist.
- **Agenda:**
  1. Review prototype coverage against pilot user journeys (learner, instructor, operations admin).
  2. Identify usability or compliance gaps that require design iterations prior to build.
  3. Rank remediation items by launch risk and effort to determine the next task for the design team.
- **Output:** Decision log capturing the selected next design task, owner, and target completion date. Store the log in the shared compliance workspace (`/Compliance/Pilot-Phase0/Prototype-Reviews`).

## Feedback Collection & Tracking
- **Feedback Channels:**
  - Figma comments aggregated before the workshop.
  - Stakeholder notes from pilot school interviews.
  - Accessibility audit findings relevant to the prototype flows.
- **Synthesis:** Product Design Lead compiles feedback into a summary doc linked from the Tier 1 backlog entry.
- **Follow-up:** Track action items in Jira Epic `PHASE0-PROTOTYPE` and update status during the weekly compliance stand-up.

## Review Cadence
- Conduct the workshop within 2 business days of publishing the prototype.
- Re-run the session after each major prototype update or prior to engineering sprint planning to confirm priorities remain valid.
